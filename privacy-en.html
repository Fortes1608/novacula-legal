Novacula Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Novacula is an app for adults aged 18 and over that analyses hair and face photos to suggest haircuts, beard styles, care routines and products. This policy explains what data we use and why.

Who we are

Controller: Lorenzo Fortes. Contact: mrlorenzo1608@gmail.com

What data we process

  • Photos you take or choose (face and hair). Face shape is estimated on your device. For the full analysis, the photos are sent to the AI provider named on the consent screen (currently Anthropic; it may be Google, and you will be told and asked again). Photos are used only to build your report, are discarded right after, and are not stored by us.
  • Your goals (length, beard, effort, style, hair colour). They stay on your device; part of them (not the hair colour) is sent with the photos for the analysis.
  • Your report, routine and reminders. Stored only on your device.
  • Purchases and subscription. Processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We receive an anonymous identifier and your subscription status, not your payment details.
  • Minimal technical data needed to run the server (for example, a count of analyses per anonymous identifier to limit abuse). We do not log photo content.

What we do not do

We do not sell data. We do not use your photos or facial attributes for advertising, tracking or model training. We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email or phone to use the app.

Legal basis and consent

We ask for your explicit consent before sending any photo to the AI provider. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Privacy; the next analysis will ask again.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data. Because your report stays on your device, you can erase everything in Settings > Delete my data. For any request, write to mrlorenzo1608@gmail.com.

Providers

Anthropic (or Google) for the AI analysis; Apple (subscriptions); RevenueCat (subscription management); Supabase (the server that brokers the analysis and enforces usage limits). These providers may process data outside your country.

Affiliate links

Some product links may be affiliate links; we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. They are labelled in the app.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way we will tell you in the app.
